Wide figures in galleries (R)

Collages (25 × 2 inches) and tall strips in galleries, as subfigures, and in a layout div. The same shapes on their own, and the fit option, are on wide and tall figures.

Wide figures open at screen height, and tall ones at screen width. The one you click opens at the spot you clicked, and stepping to another in a gallery starts it at its left end or top. In a gallery, the left and right arrow keys pan a wide figure, and at its end one more press moves to the next image; holding the key down stops at the end. On a tall strip they do nothing. The previous and next buttons step through the gallery. The viewer keeps the sides of the screen free for them, so a figure shown whole, or panned to its end, is never under a button.

The plots are drawn with these helpers:

```{r}
# A collage: `n` panels side by side, like a wide patchwork figure.
collage <- function(n = 12) {
  set.seed(1)
  par(mfrow = c(1, n), mar = c(3, 3, 2, 1))
  for (i in seq_len(n)) {
    plot(
      cumsum(rnorm(200)),
      type = "l",
      main = paste("Panel", i),
      xlab = "",
      ylab = "",
      col = hcl.colors(n)[i],
      lwd = 2
    )
  }
}

# A tall strip: `n` rows stacked, like a tall patchwork figure.
# Each seed gets its own palette (without its lightest end), so different
# strips look different.
strip <- function(seed = 2, n = 8) {
  set.seed(seed)
  palette <- c(
    "Viridis",
    "Plasma",
    "Zissou 1",
    "Dark 3",
    "Sunset",
    "Cividis",
    "Dynamic",
    "Inferno"
  )[(seed - 2) %% 8 + 1]
  par(mfrow = c(n, 1), mar = c(2, 3, 2, 1))
  for (i in seq_len(n)) {
    plot(
      cumsum(rnorm(100)),
      type = "l",
      main = paste0("Strip ", seed, ", row ", i),
      xlab = "",
      ylab = "",
      col = hcl.colors(n + 2, palette)[i],
      lwd = 2
    )
  }
}
```

1 Gallery of two collages

Two 25 × 2 inch collages in a gallery. Each opens at screen height. The left and right arrow keys and dragging pan along the collage, and at its end one more press moves to the other collage, as do the previous and next buttons.

```{r}
#| panzoom:
#|   group: collages
#| fig-width: 25
#| fig-height: 2
#| fig-alt:
#|   - >-
#|     A row of 12 small line plots of random walks, each in its own color
#|   - >-
#|     A row of 15 small line plots of random walks, each in its own color
#| fig-cap:
#|   - "A 25 × 2 inch collage of 12 random walks."
#|   - "A 25 × 2 inch collage of 15 random walks."

collage(n = 12)
collage(n = 15)
```

2 Gallery of two tall strips

Two tall strips in a gallery. Each opens at screen width and extends downwards. The left and right arrow keys do nothing, since there is nowhere to go sideways, and the previous and next buttons move between the strips.

```{r}
#| panzoom:
#|   group: strips
#| fig-width: 4
#| fig-height: 15
#| fig-alt:
#|   - "A column of 8 small line plots of random walks"
#|   - "A column of 8 small line plots of random walks"
#| fig-cap:
#|   - "A 4 × 15 inch strip of 8 random walks."
#|   - "A 4 × 15 inch strip of 8 more random walks."

strip(seed = 8, n = 8)
strip(seed = 9, n = 8)
```


6 Collages of different lengths

A long collage (40 × 2 in, 24 panels) and a shorter one (24 × 2 in, 12 panels) from two cells, joined by group. Each opens at screen height, and stepping to the other fits it afresh, from its left end, so the short collage doesn’t keep the scale or position of the long one. Only the short collage has a description, so the caption band changes height between them, and each collage stays clear of it. fig-dpi: 48 only keeps the files small.

```{r}
#| panzoom:
#|   group: mixed-collages
#| fig-width: 40
#| fig-height: 2
#| fig-dpi: 48
#| fig-alt: >-
#|   A row of 24 small line plots of random walks, each in its own color
#| fig-cap: "A 40 × 2 inch collage of 24 random walks."

collage(n = 24)
```
```{r}
#| panzoom:
#|   group: mixed-collages
#|   description: >-
#|     Each panel is a random walk of 200 steps, colored along a single palette
#|     from the first panel to the last, and titled with its panel number so the
#|     order can be read at any zoom.
#| fig-width: 24
#| fig-height: 2
#| fig-dpi: 48
#| fig-alt: >-
#|   A row of 12 small line plots of random walks, each in its own color
#| fig-cap: "A 24 × 2 inch collage of 12 random walks."

collage(n = 12)
```

Each panel is a random walk of 200 steps, colored along a single palette from the first panel to the last, and titled with its panel number so the order can be read at any zoom.
